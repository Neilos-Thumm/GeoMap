Motive

Why this exists

I aim to build a reflective and narrative photography gallery. This idea stems from the journal records I kept whenever I went on traveling or any photography trips of mine which I never had a chance to share or profoundly present them.

I want to bring those photos to life by adding whatever influenced that photography style — mainly writings I was reading at the time, historical context, or environmental factors that complement certain compositions.
