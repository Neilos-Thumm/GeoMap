About the project

The Modern Gallery

Motive

Why this exists

Every trip leaves behind more than photographs — timestamps, walking routes, quiet patterns in how a city is experienced at different hours. This gallery is an attempt to surface that data in a way that might actually be useful to someone planning their own journey through the same places.

The analytical pages here are built from real records: where I stayed, what I visited, and when. The goal is not to prescribe an itinerary but to offer an honest picture — which landmarks are genuinely walkable from which hotels, how a city's rhythm shifts across a day, and what the footprint of a visit actually looks like across a region.
